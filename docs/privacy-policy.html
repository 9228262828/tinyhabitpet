Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Tiny Habit Pet is designed to work offline. The application does not require registration, login, or the creation of a user account.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Tiny Habit Pet handles information when you use the application. We are committed to protecting user privacy and keeping the app simple, transparent, and secure.

2. Information We Collect

Tiny Habit Pet does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, payment details, advertising identifier, or social-media information.

3. No Account or Registration

The app does not require an account, registration, login, password, or online profile. You can use the main features without providing personal information.

4. Local Data Storage

Tiny Habit Pet stores limited app information locally on your device. This may include habits, completion history, streaks, pet progress, levels, coins, achievements, preferences, and settings.

This information is stored using standard local device storage and is not sent to an external server. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application may permanently remove locally stored progress.

5. Internet Connection

Tiny Habit Pet is designed to operate offline and does not require an internet connection for its core features. The app does not connect to a private backend server for storing user progress.

6. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The app does not include third-party advertising, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking, behavioral profiling, or targeted advertising.

7. Third-Party Services

Tiny Habit Pet does not use Firebase, cloud databases, cloud storage, social login, remote user profiles, or third-party analytics services.

8. Device Permissions

The current version does not require access to sensitive device permissions such as contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos, or files. Basic haptic feedback may be used when enabled in the app settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Tiny Habit Pet does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. The app contains no chat, public profiles, user-generated content, online multiplayer, social interaction, or direct messaging.

10. Data Security

Because the app does not collect or transmit personal information to external servers, personal user data is not maintained in an online database. Local information is protected by the normal security features of your device and operating system.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on the device until it is removed through the app's reset feature, the operating system's clear-data option, or application uninstallation.

12. Your Choices

You may enable or disable sound effects, haptic feedback, and dark mode from the Settings screen. You may also reset local progress from inside the application.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features, legal requirements, or privacy practices change. The latest version will remain available on this page and may also be included inside the application.

14. Contact Us

For questions, concerns, or requests related to this Privacy Policy, please use the Contact Us information provided on the application's official store listing or support page.
